The correction of handwritten exams is a time-intensive
process that lacks platforms tailored to the Spanish academic context.
Existing solutions, mostly foreign, present language barriers and
incomplete integration with national academic management systems.

This Bachelor's Thesis presents the design and implementation of the
\dfn{backend} of SCDEE (\foreignlanguage{spanish}{Sistema de Corrección
Digital de Exámenes Escritos}, Digital Handwritten Exam Correction
System), a distributed \dfn{multitenant} service that automates the
complete correction cycle from the physical delivery of scanned pages
to the final archival of the corrected exam. The system combines an
ingestion \textit{pipeline} based on \acs{qr} codes and
error-tolerant optical character recognition with a modular
architecture across thirteen Django applications and an extensible
service layer based on interchangeable \dfnpl{plugin} (authentication,
\acs{ocr} engines, zone recognisers).

From the coordinator's perspective, the system covers exam creation
with its models, declarative assignment of graders, capture of
annotations (rich text, stylus strokes, audio) and grading by rubric
or manual scoring. From the organisation manager's perspective, it
offers per-\dfn{tenant} configuration, \acs{csv} import and export,
automated closure of academic years and internationalisation of
notifications.

Validation combines an almost 90\,\% coverage on unit and integration tests,
load tests with Locust that verify \cref{rnf:concurrency} under normal
operation, and reproducible demonstration commands of the critical
\textit{pipeline}. The architecture is prepared to evolve toward
production-grade deployments, integration with \acs{lms} systems and
institutional identity federation.

\keywords{digital exam correction, multi-tenancy, OCR, Django,
distributed systems, backend architecture}
